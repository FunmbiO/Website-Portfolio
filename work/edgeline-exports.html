Edgeline Exports

Site for an online-only dealership that buys, sells and sources performance and luxury cars across Canada and the US. You can browse inventory, ask for a specific car to be found, or offer yours for sale.

What's in it

  • Three customer paths, each with its own page and form: buy from inventory, have a car sourced, or sell to the dealership.
  • The whole site switches between English and French and between light and dark themes.
  • Search and sharing are covered: structured data for the business, a generated share image, a sitemap, and a robots file that keeps the admin and API routes out of search.
  • A separate admin login for the owner, loading states, and a friendly 404 for sold or missing listings.
