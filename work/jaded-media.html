Jaded Media

The website for my own photography and film business. It opens with one line, “Photo and film, let's make art.”, then stays out of the way. Behind the public pages there's an admin area for managing content and video.

My role
Founder & developer

What's in it

  • Videos upload straight from the browser to Cloudflare R2 through short-lived signed URLs, so big files never touch the app server.
  • Security was audited in a dedicated pass: a nonce-based Content Security Policy, HSTS, clickjacking protection, a locked-down Permissions-Policy, link-scheme checks on rendered markdown, and an hourly cap on admin email sending.
  • Legal, privacy, cookie-consent and accessibility work got its own phase, and it's documented.
  • Runs on a custom domain, and every branch gets a preview deployment before anything reaches production.
