FAQ

Before you ask.

How long does a project take?

A one-page site can launch within a week. Larger builds get dates in the proposal.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Code, content and domain transfer to you at handover.

What if I need changes after launch?

Small edits come with a care plan. Anything bigger is quoted first.

Can you move my existing site?

Yes. I rebuild or migrate it and keep your URLs so search rankings carry over.

Have a site in mind?

Send a few lines on what you sell and when you want to launch. You'll get next steps and a rough quote back.
