AboutMe

Web development · Hosting · Jaded Media

I'm a big fan of convenience.

01. Mild frustration // the origin story

I can't even begin to count how many apps I've built just because something mildly annoyed me. That's most of my portfolio, honestly.

02. No subscriptions // strong opinions

I dislike subscriptions more than I can emphasize. I'd genuinely rather pay for API calls (cents, btw) than hand over $30 to $100 a month for something I could just lock in, learn and build. That's how NitroDrp happened: I got tired of paying for WeTransfer.

Truth is, I love being a developer. The internet is my oyster, and I bring that vibe to any environment I work in.

If I can't build it from scratch, there's an open source resource out there that I can modify.
